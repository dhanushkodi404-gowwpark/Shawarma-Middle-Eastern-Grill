Our Story

BORN FROM
FIRE
& PATIENCE.

Shawarma House began with a single vertical spit and an obsession: to serve the most honest shawarma in Beirut. No shortcuts, no compromises — just fire, spice, and the patience to do it right.

Our Philosophy

We believe real shawarma requires only four honest elements — meat, fire, spice, and time — and the discipline to let each one do its work. No artificial tenderizers. No gas-fired shortcuts. No compromise.

— Chef Karim Haddad
Founder & Grill Master
The Journey

Milestones Along the Way

The Beginning
First Spit Fired

Growing

What We Stand For

Our Three Non-Negotiables

These are the principles we refuse to compromise on — no matter how busy the counter gets.

Time Over Speed

Twenty-four hours of marination. Slow charcoal rotation. We believe patience is the most important ingredient in the kitchen.

Honest Ingredients

Free-range chicken and lamb, a hand-toasted seven-spice blend, and produce from local farms. Nothing artificial, ever.

Community First

We're a neighbourhood grill house. Our regulars know our names, and we know their orders. That's how it should be.
