The Craft

Four Steps to the Perfect Wrap

From spice blend to spit rotation — every step is deliberate, and every step matters.

01

Spice Marination

Free-range chicken and lamb rest for 24 hours in our seven-spice baharat blend.

02

Vertical Stacking

The marinated meat is layered by hand on the spit, alternating fat and lean.

03

Charcoal Rotation

Natural charcoal fires the spit at 480°F while it turns slowly, crisping the outer layer.

04

Carve & Serve

Freshly carved shawarma meets warm saj bread, garlic toum, and pickled turnips.
